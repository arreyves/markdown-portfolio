1. Executive Summary & Strategic Vision

Aqua Royal Water & Filtration Centre is established not merely as a retail drinking-water kiosk, but as an integrated water engineering, treatment, and distribution enterprise headquartered in Yaoundé, Cameroon. The enterprise addresses critical regional water infrastructure deficits by monetizing five interlinked revenue engines:

Engine 01

Purified Dispenser Water

High-volume 18.9L (5-gallon) bottle refills and bundled consumer water distribution for homes and offices.

Engine 02

Filtration Hardware Systems

Point-of-use (POU) and point-of-entry (POE) reverse osmosis and multimedia units for estates and businesses.

Engine 03

Maintenance & Filter Subs

Recurring replacement cartridges, sanitization schedules, membrane refreshes, and maintenance contracts.

Engine 04

Hydrogeology & Boreholes

Geophysical surveys (VES), borehole engineering oversight, and submersible pump and tank installations.

Engine 05

Institutional Water Projects

Turnkey commercial purification plants and contract water testing for schools, embassies, hotels, and healthcare clinics.

The operational strategy establishes one high-standard pilot production and service center in Yaoundé with an initial capitalization of CFA 23,430,000. Following operational validation, positive cash-flow generation, and capital recovery, the enterprise will expand across five strategic hubs in the Yaoundé metropolitan area, targeting an aggregate annual group turnover exceeding CFA 500,000,000.

Investor Capital Recovery Structure

To protect the Lead Investor's capital, the CFA 23.43 million funding is legally separated into two components:

  • Stated Paid-In Equity: CFA 5,430,000 securing Prof. Mokube's permanent 30.0% ownership interest.
  • Subordinated Shareholder Debt: CFA 18,000,000 documented via a formal Director's Loan Agreement.
  • Cash Flow Capping: Debt repayment is funded strictly from a maximum of 35% of quarterly net free cash flow after Opex and reserves are met.

Sweat Equity Vesting & Safeguards

To ensure ongoing commitment across all technical, operational, and commercial disciplines:

  • Vesting Schedule: A standard 36-month straight-line vesting schedule with a 12-month cliff applies to all four operational partners.
  • Forfeiture Clause: In the event an operational partner departs prior to the 12-month cliff, unvested equity forfeits back to the Expansion Reserve.
  • Dividend Rights: Once the shareholder debt is retired, 100% of distributed net profits are shared according to the cap table.

7. Technical & Operational Risk Protocols (Yaoundé Context)

Crystalline Basement Hydrogeology (Dry-Hole Mitigation)

Yaoundé sits on granite-gneiss basement formations where fracture systems dictate water yield. To eliminate dry-hole financial losses on client projects, Dr. Ivo Arrey mandates a 2-point Vertical Electrical Sounding (VES) resistivity survey and structural analysis before any drilling subcontract is finalized.

Source Water Quality Variations (Low pH, Iron & Microbial Risks)

Shallow water tables in the Centre Region frequently demonstrate acidic pH (5.5–6.2) and elevated iron. The treatment train incorporates pre-oxidation, manganese-greensand filtration, reverse osmosis, and post-calcite remineralization to guarantee drinking water adhering strictly to ANOR and WHO physicochemical thresholds.

Electrical Grid Instability (Surge & Outage Protection)

Frequent voltage surges and grid dropouts from Eneo threaten sensitive high-pressure pumps. The pilot investment includes commercial surge protection and a dedicated diesel generator equipped with an automatic transfer switch (ATS) to maintain unbroken production.
